Shipping Policy

Trinity Global · Limitless IOT products and services

Effective date: 31 August 2026 · Website: trinityglobal.co.za · Email: info@trinityglobal.co.za

1. Delivery areas

We ship to the delivery address provided during checkout. Please ensure your shipping information is accurate and complete to avoid delays.

2. Shipping costs

  • Free shipping on all orders of R1,000 or more.
  • For orders below R1,000, shipping charges are calculated at checkout using Bob Go courier rates.

3. Delivery times

  • Standard delivery time is 1 to 2 business days from the date your order is dispatched.
  • Delivery times may vary slightly depending on your location and courier availability.

4. Warranty returns

If you need to return a product under warranty:

  • Return shipping is completely free of charge.
  • We will arrange for a courier to collect the package from you.
  • The customer is responsible for securely packaging the item before collection.
  • Collection and return freight costs will be covered by us.

6. Contact us

If you have any questions regarding shipping, delivery, or warranty returns, please contact our support team for assistance at info@trinityglobal.co.za.

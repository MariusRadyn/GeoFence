1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Trinity Global (PTY) LTD ("Trinity Global", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use Limitless IOT, our mobile application, web application, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

The Service provides fleet and asset management features that may include GPS tracking, geofencing, IoT device monitoring, operator and wages reporting, shop ordering, and related tools depending on your app version and account configuration.

By creating an account or using the Service, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

3. Information we collect

We collect information in the following categories:

3.1 Account and profile information

  • Display name, email address, and user identifier
  • Profile photo (if you choose to upload one)
  • Email verification status
  • Authentication data managed through Firebase Authentication, including sign-in method (email/password or Google Sign-In)

3.2 Location and tracking information

  • Precise GPS location when you use live tracking, geofencing, or related map features
  • Location history stored in tracking sessions, including timestamps and route points
  • Geofence zone definitions you create (boundaries, names, and related settings)
  • Distance, movement, and geofence entry/exit events derived from location data

Location data is collected only when you use features that require it and when you grant the necessary device permissions. Some tracking features may run while the app is in use or in the background, depending on your device settings and the features you enable.

3.3 IoT, device, and connectivity information

  • IoT monitor configuration, labels, and uploaded images
  • Sensor readings, logs, and historical IoT data
  • Base station settings and network-related configuration
  • Bluetooth device discovery and connection data when you connect compatible hardware
  • MQTT and local network communication metadata needed to receive device data

3.4 Operational and business information you enter

  • Operator records (names, photos, and related workforce data you add)
  • Wages logs, summaries, and exported reports
  • Shop product listings, orders, and order history (where enabled)
  • App settings such as voice prompts, diesel pricing, logging intervals, and connected device preferences

3.5 Photos and files

  • Images you capture or select for profile pictures, operators, monitors, or shop items
  • Spreadsheet or report files generated or exported from the app on your device

3.6 Technical and usage information

  • Device type, operating system, app version, and language settings
  • IP address, browser type, and general connection information on web
  • Firebase App Check signals used to help protect the Service from abuse
  • Error, diagnostic, and security-related logs needed to maintain and secure the Service

3.7 Information we do not collect

We do not intentionally collect sensitive categories of data such as health information, government ID numbers, or payment card details through the core Service unless you voluntarily include such information in free-text fields you create (for example, in notes or operator records). Please avoid entering unnecessary sensitive information.

4. How we collect information

  • Directly from you when you register, sign in, create zones, add operators, upload photos, place orders, or change settings.
  • Automatically when the app reads device location, receives IoT/MQTT/Bluetooth data, or generates logs needed to operate features you use.
  • From third-party sign-in providers such as Google when you choose Google Sign-In (for example, name, email address, and profile image supplied by the provider).
  • From cloud infrastructure that stores and synchronizes your account data across devices.

5. How we use your information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your account and authenticate access
  • Provide tracking, mapping, geofencing, alerts, and history features
  • Display Google Maps, routes, and location-based reports
  • Store and present IoT monitor data, base station configuration, and device status
  • Support operator, wages, reporting, and shop features you enable
  • Send local notifications and optional voice prompts configured in your settings
  • Protect the Service against fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
  • Maintain, debug, and improve performance, reliability, and security
  • Respond to support requests and legal obligations

We do not use your personal information for third-party advertising or sell your personal information.

6. Legal bases for processing (EEA/UK users)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we process personal data on these bases:

  • Contract: to provide the Service you request
  • Legitimate interests: to secure, maintain, and improve the Service
  • Consent: where required for location, notifications, camera, Bluetooth, or similar device permissions
  • Legal obligation: where we must comply with applicable law

You may withdraw consent for permission-based processing in your device or browser settings, though some features may no longer work.

7. Device permissions

Depending on the features you use, the app may request permissions including:

  • Location — for maps, tracking, and geofencing
  • Camera and photos — for profile and item images
  • Notifications — for alerts and service messages on your device
  • Bluetooth — to discover and connect to nearby IoT hardware
  • Storage / files — to save or export reports and images on your device
  • Internet and network access — to sync data and use cloud features

You can manage most permissions in your device settings. Denying a permission may limit related functionality.
